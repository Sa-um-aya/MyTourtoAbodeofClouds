Explore Journey

Your 5-Day Journey

Through mist-covered hills and living root bridges

1
Shillong

Arrival & City Exploration

Begin your journey in Shillong, the "Scotland of the East." Visit Ward's Lake for a peaceful stroll, explore the vibrant Police Bazaar, and witness the stunning sunset at Shillong Peak. End your day with traditional Khasi cuisine at a local restaurant.

Ward's Lake Shillong Peak Local Cuisine
2
Cherrapunji

The Wettest Place on Earth

Journey to Cherrapunji and witness the majestic Nohkalikai Falls, India's tallest plunge waterfall. Explore the mysterious Mawsmai Cave and walk across the Seven Sisters Falls viewpoint. Marvel at the endless valleys draped in clouds.

Nohkalikai Falls Mawsmai Cave Seven Sisters
3
Mawlynnong & Dawki

Asia's Cleanest Village

Visit Mawlynnong, renowned as Asia's cleanest village, and walk across the living root bridge. Continue to Dawki to witness the crystal-clear Umngot River where boats appear to float in air. Experience the pristine beauty of the Indo-Bangladesh border region.

Living Root Bridge Dawki River Clean Village
4
Nongriat

Double Decker Root Bridge Trek

Embark on an adventurous trek to the famous Double Decker Living Root Bridge in Nongriat village. Descend 3,500 steps through lush rainforest, cross crystal streams, and discover this marvel of bioengineering. Cool off at the natural pool beneath the bridge.

3500 Steps Root Bridge Natural Pool
5
Elephant Falls & Departure

Final Memories

Visit the enchanting Elephant Falls, a three-tiered waterfall surrounded by dense forests. Shop for local handicrafts and bamboo products at the markets. Savor your last moments in Meghalaya before your departure, carrying memories of clouds and cascades.

Elephant Falls Shopping Souvenirs
🌧️

Best Time to Visit

October to May offers pleasant weather. June to September brings heavy monsoons and maximum waterfalls.

🚗

Getting Around

Hire a private cab for comfort. Shared taxis are available but less flexible. Most roads are mountainous.

🏨

Accommodation

Options range from luxury resorts to homestays. Book in advance during peak season for best choices.

🎒

What to Pack

Rain gear, comfortable trekking shoes, light woolens, camera, and respect for local culture and nature.
